About St. Canice Catholic Church

Father Shanahan purchased the lot where St. Canice Church now stands in 1851 and built Nevada City's first church there, a wood-framed building he named St. Peter's after his former parish in Troy, New York. Fire destroyed that church in 1856, and its replacement burned again in 1863. In 1864, Father John Griffin rebuilt the church in brick and renamed it St. Canice, after St. Canice Cathedral in Kilkenny, Ireland, his birthplace.

Mission Statement

Our Mission as the Parish of St. Canice Catholic Church is to proclaim the teaching and wisdom of Jesus Christ throughout our entire community by our life and participation in the Sacraments and traditions of His Holy Catholic Church.
